How it works

Everything happens on your PC, in four steps.

How it works

1
You speakHold Ctrl+Win and talk, or turn on hands-free. The microphone is only on while the bubble shows.
2
Your PC listensA speech-recognition model (Whisper) turns the sound into words on this PC: on the processor, or on the graphics card in Advanced mode.
3
It's tidied upPunctuation, capitals, paragraphs and your corrections, by built-in rules. In Advanced mode a small AI on your graphics card helps, also on this PC.
4
It's typed for youThe text appears where your cursor is, and whatever you had copied is put back.

Your privacy

✓
Offline by designDictation works fully with Wi-Fi off. Ghost Writer only goes online to check once a day for a new version (you can turn that off) and, if you choose, to download Advanced mode. Nothing about you is ever sent.
✓
No cloudYour voice and your words are never uploaded anywhere. The sound is kept in memory only until it is typed, then thrown away.
✓
No accounts, no trackingNo sign-up, no ads, no analytics, nothing reported back. Nobody, including us, sees what you say.
✓
Hands off what isn't yoursIt never reads password boxes, and only edits right after it typed (its own text, or a grammar fix in the last few sentences).

What's kept, only on your PC

✓
Your last 50 dictationsSo you can copy them again, and so formatting learns your names and style. Clear them any time.
✓
Your notes, dictionary and settingsThe Notepad, your words to listen for, and your choices, like the writing style and theme.
✓
All in one folder you controlIt sits in your own Windows profile. Open it from the app's About tab and delete anything whenever you like.

Standard and Advanced

Ghost Writer works on any Windows PC. If yours has an NVIDIA graphics card, Advanced makes it even sharper.

● Standard mode

Every PC

Fast, accurate dictation on the processor, with punctuation, capitals, paragraphs, writing styles and spoken corrections. Included in the download.

⚡ Advanced mode

NVIDIA graphics cards

A much bigger speech model for accents and fast speech, plus a small AI that fixes grammar and lays out your writing, all on your graphics card. A one-time 4.8 GB download from inside the app, only if you choose it.
