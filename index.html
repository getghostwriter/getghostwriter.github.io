Our promise

The rules Ghost Writer follows today, and every app we ship after it.

On your PC

Ghost Writer runs on your PC: your voice and words never leave it, to us or anyone else.

No ads, no analytics

Ghost Writer has no analytics: the only thing we learn is from the key check, such as how many PCs used it each day. This website sets no cookies of its own and loads nothing from other sites.

Straight answers

We say what's in beta, what goes online and why Windows warns about a new app, before you install.

You're in charge

Every setting is yours to change, and everything an app keeps is visible and removable.
