Dictation

Talk. It types. Anywhere.

Hold Ctrl+Win, say it, let go: your words appear where your cursor is, in any app. Or turn on hands-free and just talk, and it types each time you pause.

  • Punctuation, capitals and paragraphs come out right, without saying "comma".
  • Change your mind mid-sentence: "meet Tuesday, no, Wednesday" comes out as Wednesday. Ums and repeats are cleaned up.
  • Five writing styles, from Relaxed for chat to Formal for letters, and Caveman for exactly what you said.
  • Instant: the microphone opens the moment you press, so your first word is never cut off.
Smart

It knows what you're writing about.

Ghost Writer reads the text already in the box you're dictating into, so it spells the names and terms that are already there, like Siobhan, Okonkwo or Kubernetes, the way you do.

  • Names already in the box come out right 47 times in 48 in our tests, compared with about half before.
  • Matches your tone: dictate into a casual lowercase chat and your words come out casual too.
  • Your dictionary: add the names and jargon you use, and it listens out for them.
Rewrite

Fix and rewrite anything, anywhere.

Select text in any app and a small glowing ghost appears beside it. Click it, and choose.

  • Quick fixes on every PC: spelling and grammar, more formal, and letter case, instantly and without AI.
  • With Advanced mode's AI: make it shorter, friendlier or more formal, turn it into an email, meeting notes or a summary.
  • You decide: you see the result first, and your text only changes when you click Replace.
Voice commands Beta

"Hey Ghost." Your PC, on command.

Turn on always listening and give orders without touching the keyboard. Say it your own way: Ghost Writer works out what you mean, and does what it understands.

Hey Ghost, start hands-free Hey Ghost, make this shorter Hey Ghost, start a new note called Groceries Hey Ghost, switch to advanced mode Hey Ghost, dark mode Hey Ghost, new paragraph
  • A soft tick and a faint ghost tell you it heard "Hey Ghost". Nothing else it hears is kept or acted on.
  • Commands while you dictate: new paragraph, new line, scratch that, lists, and "stop" for hands-free.
  • Off by default. Switch it on when you want it, and Ghost Writer remembers.
Learns

Fix it once. It remembers.

When Ghost Writer gets a word wrong and you correct it, it learns. One correction is enough: next time it hears it right, in every app.

  • A faint thinking ghost shows for a moment when it learns something new.
  • Only real fixes count: rewording or adding words is just editing, and is ignored.
  • You're in charge: see everything it learned in Settings, and remove anything.
Your stats

Watch the hours come back.

See how many words you've dictated, how fast you speak, your streaks, and how much time you've saved compared with typing. Counted on your PC only, never what you said.

  • Time saved against typing, every day.
  • A year of activity at a glance.

Standard and Advanced

Ghost Writer works on any Windows PC. If yours has a graphics card (NVIDIA, AMD or Intel), Advanced makes it even sharper.

● Standard mode

Every PC

Fast, accurate dictation on the processor, with punctuation, paragraphs, writing styles, corrections, Quick fixes and voice commands. Choose Light, Balanced or Most accurate to suit your PC. Included in the download.

⚡ Advanced mode

NVIDIA, AMD and Intel graphics cards

A much bigger speech model for accents and fast speech, plus a small AI that fixes grammar, lays out your writing and rewrites anything you select, all on your graphics card. A one-time download from inside the app (3.3 to 4.8 GB, depending on your card), only if you choose it.

Private, by design

Everything happens on your PC. Nothing you say leaves it.
