Our promise

Ghost Media Works makes software for Windows that does its work on your own PC. These are the rules Ghost Writer follows today, and every app we ship after it.

On your PC

Ghost Writer runs on your PC: your voice and words never leave it, to us or anyone else.

No ads, no analytics scripts

Ghost Writer has no analytics: the only thing we learn is from the key check, such as how many PCs used it each day. This website runs no analytics scripts, sets no cookies and loads nothing from other sites.

Straight answers

We say what's in beta, what goes online and why Windows warns about a new app, before you install.

You're in charge
