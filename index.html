Our promise

Nothing you say leaves your PC.

Ghost Media Works makes software for Windows that does its work on your own PC. These are the rules Ghost Writer follows today, and every app we ship after it.

  • On your PCGhost Writer runs on your PC: your voice and words never leave it, to us or anyone else.
  • No ads, no analytics scriptsNo usage data about what you do. The only thing its key check shows us is how many PCs used it each day. This website sets no cookies and loads nothing from other sites.
  • Straight answersWe say what's in beta, what goes online and why Windows warns about a new app, before you install.
  • You're in chargeEvery setting is yours to change, and everything an app keeps is visible and removable.
