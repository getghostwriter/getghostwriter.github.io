Dictation

Talk. It types. Anywhere.

Hold Ctrl+Win and talk, or press Win+Alt for hands-free. Words appear where your cursor is.

More
  • Hold Ctrl+Win, say it, let go: your words appear where your cursor is, in almost any app. Or press Win+Alt for hands-free and it types each time you pause.
  • Punctuation, capitals and paragraphs come out right, without saying "comma".
  • Um and uh are left out, and sound-alike slips like "park in the garbage" become "garage".
  • A slim listening bar shows your voice as one flowing line while it listens.
  • Live words: switch on Show my words as I speak (Settings > Dictation) to watch them appear as you talk, put right as soon as you stop.
  • Your microphone: pick the one Ghost Writer listens to, or let it follow Windows' choice, and set each sound's volume (Settings > Sound & microphone).
  • Your way of writing: spaces, periods, numbers as digits or words, lists and email greetings, all in Settings.

Gets names right

It knows what you're writing about.

Names already in the box, like Siobhan or Kubernetes, come out spelled your way.

More
  • Ghost Writer reads the text already in the box you're dictating into, so names and terms that are already there, like Siobhan or Kubernetes, come out spelled your way.
  • Fits the app: it notices chat, email, documents or code and writes to suit.
  • Your dictionary: add the names and jargon you use, and it listens out for them.
  • Screen context (optional): it can also use the words in the window you're dictating into. Off until you turn it on, and never saved.

Your AI · beta

Stay local, or bring your own AI.

Ghost Writer works fully on your PC. Want more help? Connect an AI you've installed and signed into, and choose what it can read.

More
  • Your choice: nothing is connected until you sign in and choose to use it. Turn it off, or just pause it, any time.
  • A switch per area: stats, settings, notes, to-dos and calendar, reminders, voice notes, past dictations, your words, voice commands, file names, Help and more. An area you switch off is never read.
  • It asks first before it deletes, opens a file, cleans up or runs a risky command. Ask for several steps and it shows its plan; you say Go once.
  • Helps across the app: AI rewrite, voice-note AI and Help answers use your AI first, then Advanced mode's AI on your PC if it can't answer and you have it.
  • Whole PC stays off unless you turn it on, and Ghost Writer asks before it does.
  • What you ask it, and what the areas you leave on let it read, is routed through its company. Your dictation is routed too only if you turn on Proofread my dictation (Settings > AI). Your recordings never are.

Search your PC

Find anything. Then free up space.

Press Alt+Shift+S from any app, type a few letters, and open what you mean.

More
  • Press Alt+Shift+S from any app, or say "Hey Ghost, search my PC", type a few letters, and open the app, Windows setting, folder or file you mean. Drag a result straight into another app.
  • Apps, Settings, folders and files in one box, as you type, closest matches first. Mistype a word and Related results show the files you probably meant.
  • Clean up shows each drive's free space, safe junk (temporary files, caches, thumbnails, crash reports) and your biggest files. It's built to leave your files, history, passwords and sign-ins alone, and it shows what it will remove first.
  • No administrator needed, except one OK from Windows if you tick Windows leftovers in Clean up. Searching happens on your PC. If you connect your own AI and leave its Search your PC switch on, file and folder names (never contents) are routed through that company.

Sync between your PCs

Your notes and stats on your other PC.

Move your data to another PC on your key, or keep one stat board across your PCs. End-to-end encrypted.

More
  • Move my data: only when you press Send. Your notes, words, stats and settings are locked with a code; type it on your other PC within 15 minutes. No internet? Save to a file instead (Settings > General).

Standard and Advanced

Any PC. Sharper with a graphics card.

Standard comes in the download. Advanced needs an NVIDIA, AMD or Intel graphics card.

More
  • Ghost Writer works on any PC with Windows 10 (version 1809 or newer) or Windows 11. With a graphics card, Advanced makes it sharper.
  • Standard mode, every PC: included in the download. Dictation on the processor, with punctuation, paragraphs, writing styles, quick fixes, voice commands and voice notes. Choose Light, Balanced or Most accurate to suit your PC. Connect your own AI for AI rewrite and voice-note AI.
  • Advanced mode, NVIDIA, AMD and Intel graphics cards: a much bigger speech model for accents and fast speech, plus a small AI for grammar, paragraphs by topic, rewriting, voice-note summaries and searching inside documents. Needs a graphics card with 4 GB of its own memory or more (6 GB for the AI too). A one-time download from inside the app, 3.3 to 4.8 GB.
