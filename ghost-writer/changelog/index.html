Ghost Writer 6.8

What's new

Every update, in plain words. Ghost Writer checks for a new version once a day and offers it with one click; your notes and settings are kept.

Version 6.8

Voice notes, Train My Ghost, and a fresh new look.

  • New: Voice notes (beta): record, replay and search what you said; summaries and to-dos need Advanced.
  • New: Train My Ghost: fix a voice note's unsure words (each note's Train button) and it learns them.
  • New: a fresh look, with a side menu of icons, cleaner pages and Settings grouped into cards.
  • New: a new look for the Ghost himself: smoky grey with glowing indigo eyes, everywhere he appears.
  • New: a smaller, smoother listening bar: your voice as one flowing line, true to how loud you speak.
  • New: Hey Ghost answers show as a small card; a green check only when something was done.
  • New: a short logo animation plays while Ghost Writer starts (Settings > App).
  • Better: Polished and Formal writing styles turn everyday speech into work-ready writing (Settings).
  • Better: drag Search your PC results into any app, or open it by saying "Hey Ghost, search my PC".
  • Better: Settings, Help and the other main pages open at once, even the first time (Settings > App).
  • Changed: About is now in Settings, with a Contact us button, and we can show you important notices.
  • Changed: Ghost Writer's website has a new home: ghostmediaworks.com/ghost-writer.
  • Fixed: misfiring voice commands, clearer dictation, smoother scrolling, faster PC search, quick voice notes.

Version 6.7

Access keys work on more PCs.

  • Fixed: the access key now checks on PCs where it couldn't before, and says why if it still can't.

Version 6.6

Free by invitation, plus Screen context for better names.

  • New: Ghost Writer is free by invitation; your access key is checked online and works 3 days offline.
  • New: Screen context reads the window you're typing in, so names and topic words come out right.
  • Better: choose how long the ghost beside selected text stays (Settings > AI).
  • Fixed: no made-up words over background sound, no lost spaces, and fewer ghost pop-ups by mistake.

Version 6.5

Clean up your drives from Search your PC.

  • New: Clean up (in Search your PC) frees disk space by removing junk and showing your biggest files.
  • Better: fold or open any Settings section by clicking its heading, and it stays that way.
  • Fixed: no made-up words after long dictations, no split greetings, and no stuck Windows key.

Version 5.9

Hey Ghost understands more, and you can make your own voice commands.

  • Hey Ghost understands commands where two s sounds run together, like close Spotify or Ghost, stop.
  • Hey Ghost, stop ends hands-free more reliably, even right at the end of a sentence. Turn off hands-free and hands-free off work too.
  • After Hey Ghost, the listening ghost waits 3.5 seconds for your command, then fades away. Say it in one breath or pause first; both work.
  • Say Ghost or Hey Ghost before any command. Ghost, open Spotify works the same as Hey Ghost, open Spotify.
  • New on the Voice commands tab: a wake word sensitivity slider, from Speak up to Ultra sensitive. The new default hears softer voices than before.
  • Hey Ghost no longer wakes when someone nearby just says Hey Gus or a ghost in a sentence.
  • New shortcuts: let go of Win+Alt to turn hands-free on or off. Hold Ctrl+Alt for a voice command. Win+Alt+R and other Win+Alt shortcuts still work.
  • Fixed: the listening ghost could stay on screen after a tiny sound, or appear from keys another program sent.
  • Fixed: with several screens, the helper ghost no longer jumps between them. It stays on the screen you're using.
  • Fixed: sound from your own speakers, or a muted microphone's faint hiss, could wake Hey Ghost. Your voice is heard as before.
  • Hey Ghost, hide works far more often, even when the speech model mishears hide.
  • Say an email or web address and it's written as one: john dot smith at gmail dot com becomes john.smith@gmail.com.
  • Improve writing now really edits: it cuts filler and wordiness and keeps your tone. Changed words show in color (Settings, AI rewrite).
  • Hey Ghost guesses a command it doesn't know and asks: Did I get that right? Say yes and it learns your words. Switch it in Voice commands.
  • New voice command diary (Voice commands tab): records every voice command for a day, on this PC only, so misses can be fixed.
  • Settings has a search box and new groups. Sounds, the microphone, hands-free timing and double-tap speed can now be changed there.
  • The Hey Ghost helper ghost is easier to see, with a slider in Settings. The ghost beside selected text has its own switch.
  • Windows now calls the app Ghost Writer, not Python: on the microphone in use icon, in Task Manager, Startup apps and notifications.
  • Pop-ups like Improve writing can be dragged anywhere on the screen.
  • New rounded search bars in Settings and Notepad that keep their shape when you click them.
  • Settings that change a look show a preview, like the helper ghost's visibility. The volume slider plays a sample sound.
  • Make your own voice commands: up to 10 phrases each, for a site, an app, keys, text or any command, picked from a scrolling list. Voice tab or Settings.
  • Say Ghost, pause, then the command: it now waits for you instead of saying it didn't understand.
  • Just say a web address after Ghost (Ghost, walmart.com) and it opens. Ghost, close amazon.com closes that browser tab.
  • Hey Ghost understands more of how the speech model writes commands, like GhostOpenAmazon.com, pull-up, or and Ghost, close Outlook.
  • Apple Music opens its web player when the app isn't on your PC. The Voice commands tab explains everything more clearly.

More ways to open and close apps by voice.

  • Say run, open, or start to launch an app. Close, kill, exit, quit, or shut down closes any app you name.

  • If Hey Ghost does not know that app, the question mark ghost comes back. Open Apple Music opens applemusic.com.

  • When a command works, the listening ghost steps aside and a card in the middle of the screen confirms it.
  • Say stop to send the listening ghost away, or turn voice commands off.
  • Hey Ghost runs play and pause right away, and a finished command is not read a second time.

Hey Ghost runs your PC: apps, timers, music and more.

  • Hey Ghost is heard more reliably. Speech that writes HeyGhost, Hey go, or He goes still counts; Hey gosh does not.
  • Hold Win+Alt or Ctrl+Alt and say a command. A faint ghost with a question mark shows it is listening.
  • Fixed: only one ghost appears when you ask for help, by keys or by saying Hey Ghost.
  • Fixed: holding Win+Alt no longer types what you said. It runs it as a Ghost command.
  • Hey Ghost runs your PC: open, switch to or close any app by name, open folders and Settings pages, go to a website, or search the web.
  • Fixed: close Google or Chrome closes Google Chrome. Minimize Google minimizes that window instead of Ghost Writer.
  • Say undo, save, select all, new tab, close the tab, go back, scroll down, zoom in, snap left, or minimize. Hey Ghost, send presses Enter.
  • Set a timer or a reminder, ask for the battery or the volume, set the volume to a level, and play, pause, or skip music in any app.
  • Fixed: Hey Ghost, unmute no longer mutes a PC that was not muted, and volume up or down now moves a step you can hear.
  • Fixed: when paste does not land, the same words are typed in. In Wizard101, press Enter to open chat first.
  • Fixed: Hey Ghost, new line or new paragraph no longer presses Enter a second time, which was making Windows play its error sound.
