Is my download real?

Ghost Writer comes only from these places. Anything else is not from us.

  • This website: ghostmediaworks.com (and our older address, getghostwriter.github.io).

If Windows or your antivirus blocks it

Antivirus blocked it

Some antivirus apps are wary of any new app that isn't signed yet. First check the file is the real one. If it matches, restore or allow it in your antivirus, and please tell us which antivirus it was, so we can report the mistake to its maker.

Smart App Control blocked it

On some Windows 11 PCs, Smart App Control blocks every app that isn't signed, with no "Run anyway" button. Ghost Writer can't run on those PCs yet. Turning Smart App Control off is your own security choice, and on many PCs it can't simply be turned back on, so we don't recommend doing it just for Ghost Writer.

It says my key can't be checked

Ghost Writer checks your key online when it starts and every hour. Without internet it keeps working for up to 3 days. If it still can't check, it says why: often a firewall or a work network blocking it.

What you need

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit

A 672 MB download and about 1 GB of space once installed. No administrator rights needed. Speech accuracy in Settings (Light, Balanced, Most accurate) suits slower or faster PCs.

A microphone, and English

Ghost Writer understands English only, for now. Any microphone: built-in, headset or USB. Windows must let desktop apps use the microphone (Settings > Privacy & security > Microphone).

For Advanced (optional)

An NVIDIA, AMD or Intel graphics card with its own memory: 4 GB or more for the bigger speech model, 6 GB or more for the AI too. A one-time download from inside the app: 4.8 GB (NVIDIA) or 3.3 GB (AMD and Intel).

Known issues

Ghost Writer 7.2.0: no known issues listed yet. Voice commands and voice notes are marked beta and still improving.

Something wrong? Tell us on the feedback page or at ghostthepatriot@gmail.com, and we'll list it here once we know.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. It's free, by invitation: request access and you get a key. No subscription, no ads. With your key you can use everything, including Advanced mode if your PC has a graphics card for it.

Does anything I say leave my PC?

No. Your voice is turned into text on your own PC, and nothing you say or write is uploaded, to us or anyone else. Dictation works with Wi-Fi off.

Ghost Writer only goes online to check your access key (the key, a scrambled id of your PC and the version), to check for a new version and, if you choose it, to download Advanced mode. Privacy policy

How do I get an access key?

Fill in the request form. We read every request, and your key arrives by email, usually within a few days. Check your spam folder too. Download Ghost Writer, open it and enter the key.

Can I use my key on more than one PC?

Yes, on up to 2 PCs. Please don't share it. To use a different key: Settings > App > Access key > Change key.

Which apps does it work in?

Almost anywhere you can type: email, Word and Google Docs, chat apps, your browser, code editors and more. It's built never to type into or read password boxes.

Do I need a graphics card?

No. Standard mode works on any Windows 10 or 11 PC, using the processor. With an NVIDIA, AMD or Intel graphics card that has its own memory, you can add Advanced mode from inside the app.

How do I get updates?

Ghost Writer checks for a new version when it starts and once a day, and offers it with one click (you can turn the check off in Settings > App). Your dictations, notes, dictionary and settings are kept. See What's new.

How do I uninstall it?

Open Windows Settings, then Apps, then Installed apps (on Windows 10: Apps & features). Find Ghost Writer and choose Uninstall. It asks whether to also delete your saved dictations, notes, dictionary and settings.

Is there a Mac, Linux or phone version? Other languages?

No. Ghost Writer is for Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit), and it understands English only, for now.

I found a bug, or I have an idea. Where do I tell you?

On the feedback page, or by email at ghostthepatriot@gmail.com. Tell us what you were doing and what happened, but please never include anything private you dictated.
