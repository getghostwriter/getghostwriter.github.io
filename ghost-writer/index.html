What it does

One small app that makes your PC listen. Everything below runs on your own computer.

Dictate into almost any app

Email, documents, chat, the browser, code. Commas, periods and capitals are added for you, and um and uh are left out. Or go hands-free and it types each time you pause.

Fix and rewrite what you select

Select text anywhere and a small ghost appears: fix spelling and grammar on any PC, or with Advanced mode make it shorter, friendlier or into an email. Your text changes only when you click Replace.

"Hey Ghost" Beta

Say "Hey Ghost, open Spotify", "set a timer for ten minutes" or "next tab". Hold Ctrl+Alt instead if you'd rather not say the words. Always listening is off until you turn it on.

Voice notes and Train My Ghost Beta

Record a note, play it back and search what you said. Fix the words it wasn't sure of, and your Ghost learns them for next time, in dictation too.

Private by design

Nothing you say leaves your PC.

Your voice is turned into text on your PC. Nothing you say or type is sent to us or anyone else.

  • Works offlineDictation works with Wi-Fi off, for up to 3 days between key checks.
  • Online for four things onlyChecking your key (the key, a scrambled id of your PC and the version), checking for updates, moving your data to your other PC when you press Sync (scrambled so only your PCs can read it) and, if you choose it, downloading Advanced mode.
  • Hands off what isn't yoursIt's built never to type into or read password boxes. Optional screen context is off until you turn it on, and never saved. Privacy policy

Runs on any PC. Sharper with a graphics card.

Standard mode

Every Windows 10 and 11 PC

Fast, accurate dictation on the processor, with writing styles, quick fixes, voice commands and voice notes. Included in the download.

Advanced mode

NVIDIA, AMD and Intel graphics cards

A much bigger speech model for accents and fast speech, plus a small AI for grammar, layout and rewriting, all on your graphics card. An optional one-time download from inside the app (3.3 to 4.8 GB).

Quick answers

Is it really free?

Yes. No subscription and no ads. With your key you can use everything, including Advanced mode if your PC has a graphics card for it.

Which apps does it work in?

Almost anywhere you can type: email, Word and Google Docs, chat apps, your browser, code editors. It's built never to type into or read password boxes.

Why does Windows warn me when I install it?

Windows warns about new apps whose publisher hasn't bought a paid code-signing certificate yet. Click More info, then Run anyway. More install help, including antivirus and Smart App Control.
