Your voice workspace

Plan, hear and arrange it your way.

Notes, to-dos and a calendar in Planner. Home with the boxes you use. Text read out loud. Your own AI, if you want it.

More
  • Planner: notes, to-do lists with due dates, repeats and reminders, and a calendar. "Hey Ghost, add milk to my groceries list."
  • Home, your way: add, move and size boxes, and pick what sits in Quick settings.
  • Talk back and Read aloud: hear answers, reminders or any text you select, in a natural voice or Windows' voice.
  • Your AI (beta): connect an AI you've installed and signed into, with a switch for each area it may read. What it reads is routed through its company.
  • Sync between your PCs: move your data, or keep your stats in step, end-to-end encrypted and only when you choose.

Privacy, your way

Keep it on your PC, or bring in your AI.

Your voice becomes text on your PC, so dictation works even offline. Connect an AI when you want more help, and choose what it can see.

More
  • Dictation works with Wi-Fi off, for up to 3 days between key checks.
  • Connect your own AI and what you ask it is routed through its company; your dictation only if you turn on Proofread my dictation.
  • The key check sends only the key, a scrambled id of your PC and the version. Sync and stats run only when you choose them, scrambled so only your PCs can read them.
  • It's built never to type into or read password boxes. No ads, no analytics, no cookies. Privacy policy

Quick answers

What can it do besides typing?

Fix, rewrite or read aloud what you select, keep voice notes, plan in its Planner (notes, to-dos and a calendar), turn recordings into text, search your PC and run it with "Hey Ghost". Arrange Home with the boxes you use, and connect your own AI when you want more help. With your key you can use everything, including Advanced mode if your PC has a graphics card for it. See every feature

Which apps does it work in?

Almost anywhere you can type: email, Word and Google Docs, chat apps, your browser, code editors. It's built never to type into or read password boxes.

Why does Windows warn me when I install it?

Windows warns about new apps whose publisher hasn't bought a paid code-signing certificate yet. Click More info, then Run anyway. More install help, including antivirus and Smart App Control.
