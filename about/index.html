How we work

If a job can run on your PC, it runs on your PC.

  • Local firstOur apps go online only for what they say they do, such as checking for updates.
  • No ads, no analytics scriptsWe don't sell data or show ads. Ghost Writer sends no usage data about what you do. The only thing its key check shows us is how many PCs used it each day. This website runs no analytics scripts, sets no cookies and loads nothing from other sites.
  • Honest about the rough edgesBeta means beta. We list what's new and what's known to be wrong, and we explain warnings before you meet them.
  • Built with the people who use itGhost Writer is free by invitation while it grows, and shaped by the feedback of the people using it.

Ghost Writer goes online for four things only: checking your key (the key, a scrambled id of your PC and the version), checking for updates, moving your data to your other PC when you press Sync (scrambled so only your PCs can read it) and, if you choose it, downloading Advanced mode.
