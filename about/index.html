How we work

Fast on your PC. Online when you choose.

Dictation runs on your PC. Ghost Writer goes online for your key, updates, Sync and stats, and for your own AI if you connect one.

More
  • Key check: the key, a scrambled id of your PC and the version.
  • Updates: new versions, Advanced mode or the natural voices if you choose them.
  • Sync: your data moves to your other PC only when you press Send, end-to-end encrypted so only your PCs can read it.
  • Stats: only while Keep stats in step on my PCs is switched on, encrypted the same way.
  • Your own AI is your choice, and only if you connect it on the Your AI page: what you ask it, and what you let it read, is routed through that company. Your voice never is.
  • No ads, no analytics scripts. We don't sell data. Ghost Writer sends no usage data about what you do; the key check tells us which version each PC runs and how many PCs used it each day; a message's Got it adds one to its count.
  • This website runs no analytics scripts, sets no cookies and loads nothing from other sites.
  • Beta means beta: we list what's new and what's known to be wrong, and explain warnings before you meet them.
  • Ghost Writer is by invitation while it grows, and shaped by the feedback of the people using it.
