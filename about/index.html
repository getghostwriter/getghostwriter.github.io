How we work

If a job can run on your PC, it runs on your PC.

Ghost Writer goes online for four things only. Nothing you say leaves your PC.

More
  • Key check: the key, a scrambled id of your PC and the version.
  • Updates: new versions, and Advanced mode if you choose it.
  • Sync: your data moves to your other PC only when you press Sync, scrambled so only your PCs can read it.
  • Stats: only if you keep your stats in step between your PCs, scrambled the same way.
  • No ads, no analytics scripts. We don't sell data. Ghost Writer sends no usage data about what you do; the key check shows us only how many PCs used it each day.
  • This website runs no analytics scripts, sets no cookies and loads nothing from other sites.
  • Beta means beta: we list what's new and what's known to be wrong, and explain warnings before you meet them.
  • Ghost Writer is free by invitation while it grows, and shaped by the feedback of the people using it.
